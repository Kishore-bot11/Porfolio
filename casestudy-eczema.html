EczemaClinic UK — Full Website Redesign

Website Redesign · 2025

Project Summary

Led a complete website redesign for EczemaClinic UK, transforming the brand’s digital presence with a modern, user-centric layout that improves clarity, trust, and engagement. The redesign focused on enhancing user experience, visual hierarchy, accessibility, and content relevance for both patients and healthcare professionals.

My role

UI/UX Designer & Web Redesign Specialist

What I Improved

💡Holistic Visual Redesign: Reimagined the site with a clean, professional aesthetic optimized for readability and emotional comfort, appropriate for a healthcare audience
💡Improved Navigation & Structure: Streamlined the information architecture to ensure visitors can easily find services, treatment guides, and contact information
💡Enhanced Trust & Authority: Designed clear sections for clinical expertise, treatment solutions, and patient support to reinforce credibility
💡User-Centered Layouts: Applied thoughtful typography, spacing, and visual cues to reduce clutter and support accessibility
💡Mobile-Responsive Optimization: Ensured consistent experience across devices with responsive breakpoints and adaptive elements
💡Clear Calls-to-Action: Strategically placed CTAs throughout the site to encourage inquiries, appointment bookings, and patient engagement
💡Visual Storytelling: Used relevant imagery and icons to support comprehension and build an empathetic connection with users
💡​​​​​​​Content Hierarchy & Readability: Organized content into digestible sections with clear headings, supporting patient education and easy scanning

Outcome

The redesigned website now delivers a more intuitive and trustworthy user experience, increases engagement through clear visual direction, and reinforces the clinic’s expertise in dermatological care. The result is a site that feels welcoming, informative, and professional—fit for a healthcare brand with a mission of support and education.
