About

Design is thinking
made visual.

I'm a UI/UX and Product Designer based in Chennai, India with 2+ years of experience building user-focused digital products across web, mobile, and brand.

My process starts with empathy and research — understanding the real problem before touching a pixel. I then use structured frameworks like Jobs-to-be-Done and double-diamond to move from insight to solution.

I believe great design is invisible — when it works perfectly, users don't notice the craft, they just feel at ease. That's the standard I hold myself to.

How I Work

My design process.

01
Discover
User interviews, competitive analysis, heuristic evaluation — understanding the landscape before designing.
02
Define
Synthesising research into clear problem statements, personas, and user journey maps.
03
Ideate
Rapid sketching, concept exploration, and collaborative workshops to generate solutions.
04
Design
High-fidelity UI, interaction patterns, and design systems — pixel-perfect, production-ready.
05
Validate
Usability testing, A/B feedback, and iteration cycles to confirm the solution actually works.
06
Deliver
Dev handoff with annotated specs, component libraries, and close collaboration through build.
