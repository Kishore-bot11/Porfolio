Zero Waste — App Screens & User Research

App Design · Research · 2024

Overview

ZeroWaste is a mobile platform that connects households and small businesses with scrap collectors and buyers. The app simplifies the process of selling, donating, renting, and purchasing used or recyclable items, promoting a more sustainable and circular economy.

Challenge

Many reusable and recyclable items end up as waste because users lack an easy way to connect with buyers or collectors. Existing solutions often provide limited transparency, poor tracking, and fragmented user experiences.

My role - UI/UX Designer

📱Designed the complete mobile app experience
📋Created user flows and wireframes
🎨Designed the visual interface and design system
🧭Structured buyer, seller, and rental journeys
🎯Optimized the experience for accessibility and ease of use

Design Approach

The app was designed around simplicity and sustainability. I focused on reducing friction throughout the user journey, making it easy for users to list items, request pickups, track collections, and discover reusable products through a clean and intuitive interface.

Visual Direction

The interface uses a nature-inspired green color palette to reinforce the sustainability mission. Clean layouts, intuitive navigation, and clear action-driven screens help users complete tasks quickly and confidently.

Outcome

ZeroWaste transforms waste management into a seamless digital experience. By connecting sellers, buyers, and collectors in one platform, the app encourages responsible disposal, promotes reuse, and supports a more sustainable community.
