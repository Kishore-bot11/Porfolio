Design Studio - Website Landing Page

Agency · 2024–2026

Overview

Design Studio is a modern agency website designed to showcase creative services, build credibility, and generate client inquiries. The focus was on creating a clean, visually engaging experience that highlights the studio's expertise in UI/UX, branding, and digital design.

Goal

To create a professional online presence that effectively communicates services, showcases work, and encourages potential clients to start a project.

Role

UI/UX Designer

Responsibilities

  • UX Research
  • Wireframing
  • UI Design
  • Responsive Design
  • Interactive Prototyping

Design Approach

The website follows a clear user journey from introduction to conversion through strategically structured sections including services, process, case studies, testimonials, and contact CTAs. A modern visual style, strong typography, and clean layouts were used to enhance readability and user engagement.

Outcome

The final design delivers a polished and conversion-focused experience that strengthens brand credibility while making it easy for visitors to explore services and connect with the studio.
