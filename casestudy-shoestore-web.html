Shoe Store — Web

Web · E-commerce · 2024

Overview

SHO is a concept sneaker and lifestyle e-commerce platform designed to deliver a premium online shopping experience. The project focused on creating a visually striking website that blends modern fashion aesthetics with seamless product discovery.

Challenge

Most fashion e-commerce websites struggle to balance branding and usability. The challenge was to create a high-impact shopping experience that captures attention while helping users quickly explore collections and products.

My role - UI/UX Designer

🖥️Designed the complete desktop experience
📋Created user flows and page structure
🎴Designed promotional and campaign sections
🧩Established visual hierarchy and UI components
↔️Ensured responsive design consistency

Design Approach

The design was built around bold visuals, immersive product showcases, and clear conversion paths. Large promotional banners, curated collections, and strategic CTAs were used to guide users naturally through the shopping journey.

Outcome

The final design transforms the shopping journey into a visually engaging experience that strengthens brand identity, improves product discoverability, and encourages users to explore, engage, and purchase with confidence.
