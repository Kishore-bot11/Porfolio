MicroRentals — Website Revamp

UI/UX Overhaul · 2025

Overview

MicroRentals provides IT rental and managed services solutions across Australia. I redesigned the entire website to create a modern, user-friendly experience that better communicates the brand's expertise and services.

My role - UI/UX Designer

🔬Conducted UX audit and research
🖌️Redesigned the full website
🪄Improved navigation and content structure
💻Created responsive UI designs
🎨Built a consistent design system

Challenges

The website contained a large amount of content and service offerings, making navigation difficult. Users had to spend extra time understanding what the company offered and where to find relevant solutions.

Key Improvements

🎯Cleaner and more professional visual design
🎯Simplified navigation and service discovery
🎯Stronger call-to-actions across the website
🎯Better content organization and readability
🎯Fully responsive experience for all devices

Impact

The redesign transformed a content-heavy website into a modern and intuitive platform. Users can now explore services more easily, understand the value proposition faster, and engage with the business more effectively.
